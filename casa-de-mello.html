The Retreat | A Casa with a Memory

Built circa 1863
Portuguese Goan history, restored.

Casa De Mello dates back to the 1860s. Its walls carry the traces of colonial heritage, while its proportions, materials, and quiet details speak to the life of a Goan home.

Today, Casa has been thoughtfully reimagined as a Ridhira Retreat. Its history remains present, but the experience is made for now: slower mornings, restorative rituals, generous meals, and time that feels entirely your own.

What Awaits

Architecture
as Character

Casa De Mello is not designed to feel new. Its character comes from what has endured—the texture of its walls, the quiet of its rooms, the relationship between inside and outside, and the details that reveal the history of the home.

Every space is considered with care, without losing its sense of age. The result is a retreat that feels intimate, rooted, and unmistakably of Goa.

The Best of Goa,
Close By.

Set in the serene town of Saligao, Casa De Mello places you close to the best of North Goa while keeping you away from its rush. Candolim’s beaches, Calangute’s energy and Baga’s nightlife are just a short drive away.

For those drawn to heritage and architecture, Goa’s churches and historic quarters offer stories at every turn. Cafés, local markets, art galleries, and cultural trails are all within easy reach.
