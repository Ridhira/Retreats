The Tiger Hills

Tiger Hills brings together the parts of a good night that are usually scattered across the city: a café for the first conversation, a rooftop for sunset, The Club for when the evening gathers pace, rooms for landing well, and Ode Spa for the morning after.

It is not a conventional hotel. It is a compact, considered place to meet, celebrate, stay over, and begin again.

The Club at Tiger Hills

Where the Evening Finds Its Pace.

The Club is Tiger Hills’ social centre: a place for dinner that runs late, music that changes the room, cocktails made with intent, and nights that do not need an occasion.

It begins with the people at the table and gathers energy as the evening moves on. Some nights are made for a crowd. Others are best shared with a few familiar faces.

  • Rooftop sunsets and late drinks.
  • Dinner-to-music evenings.
  • Curated DJ, guitar, string, jazz, and live-music formats.
  • Signature cocktails and a property-specific drinks trail.
  • Private tables, celebrations, and limited takeovers.

ODE SPA AT TIGER HILLS

Reset, at Your Own Pace.

Ode Spa at Tiger Hills is designed for the moments before and after the night. A quick grooming ritual before dinner. A head massage after a late set. A slower reset when the morning asks for less. With two spa rooms, the experience is intimate, efficient, and made to fit the rhythm of your stay.
