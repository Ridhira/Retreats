Udaipur, Rajasthan  ·  Aravalli Foothills

Tiger
Hills

Stillness above the City of Lakes.

The Retreat

The hills above
Udaipur hold a
different silence.

Tiger Hills sits on a ridge in the Aravalli foothills, looking out over Udaipur's famous lakes — Pichola, Fateh Sagar, the City Palace reflected in early morning light. The retreat is twelve intimate rooms built into the landscape, not imposed upon it.

At Tiger Hills, time feels different. The altitude softens the heat. The view slows your thinking. And the absence of traffic, notifications, and noise makes space for something most guests have forgotten: genuine rest.

What Awaits

Grounded in
the Aravallis

01

Sunrise Yoga & Meditation

Practice at the edge of the ridge as the sun rises over Lake Pichola. The elevation and the silence make this unlike any yoga class you have attended indoors.

02

Aravalli Nature Walks

Guided walks through ancient rocky terrain with a naturalist who has spent decades studying the Aravallis. Medicinal plants, migratory birds, and geological history.

03

Rajasthani Thali Ritual

Each evening meal is a ceremonial thali — seasonal, vegetarian, and prepared according to local marwari traditions. Eaten slowly. Together. As the city glows below.

Wellness Partner

Ode Spa
at Tiger Hills

Our Ode Spa at Tiger Hills is designed around Rajasthani healing traditions — rose, saffron, and desert botanicals form the base of treatments that have been adapted by our therapists over years of practice in this specific terrain.

The treatment rooms face the Aravallis. Many guests choose to finish a session and simply sit — watching the hills, feeling the stillness, letting the treatment settle.

Udaipur, Rajasthan  ·  Ancient Haveli Estate

Karohi Haveli

The furthest you can get. The closest you will feel.

The Retreat

A Rajput estate
that time chose
to leave alone.

Karohi Haveli is Ridhira's most secluded retreat — a centuries-old Rajput haveli set within a private forest outside Udaipur. Ancient banyan trees whose roots predate the haveli. Dry-stone walls. A well that still draws water from the same aquifer it always has.

There are eight rooms here. No pool. No gym. No concierge activities board. What Karohi Haveli offers instead is rarer: genuine undisturbed silence, clean forest air, and the slow, deliberate pace that deep restoration actually requires.

What Awaits

Slower.
Deeper. Truer.

01

Panchakarma Detox

A structured 5–21 day Ayurvedic detox programme designed for genuine cellular restoration. Karohi Haveli is one of the few retreat environments in India suitable for a full Panchakarma protocol.

02

Maun — The Practice of Silence

Guided programmes in conscious silence. Not monastic austerity — but a structured, supported practice of turning inward, available for half-days, full-days, or entire stays.

03

Longevity Protocols

Integrated with Ode Life — Ridhira's longevity membership programme — Karohi Haveli offers biomarker assessments, Ayurgenomics consultations, and personalised longevity planning for Ode Life members.

Wellness & Longevity

Ode Spa meets
Ode Life at Karohi Haveli

Karohi Haveli is the only Ridhira property where Ode Spa and Ode Life — Ridhira's longevity membership programme — are fully integrated. Ode Life members can access biomarker panels, Ayurgenomics consultations, and AI-assisted health passport updates during their stay.

For non-members, Karohi Haveli offers Ode Spa's full Panchakarma-adjacent wellness programmes — designed for those who want more than relaxation. More than a spa holiday. Something that actually changes how they feel six months later.
