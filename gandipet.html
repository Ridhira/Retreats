Ridhira Retreat, Gandipet

A quiet corner of Hyderabad, set on the water.

On the shores of Gandipet lake, two and a half acres of lawns, mature trees and gardens run right down to the water. It is close enough to the city to reach in minutes, and quiet enough to feel like you have left it behind.

Every space here, indoors and out, is shaped around the lake and the trees — built around them, never over them — so a celebration feels less like an event and more like an escape.

The Property

A Lakefront Sanctuary in Hyderabad

Gandipet Lake

Ridhira Retreat, Gandipet brings together an open lawn on the water, a timber-and-glass banquet hall and a Bali-themed villa with its own pool — one lakeshore that can hold an intimate dinner or a wedding for a thousand.

Shaded paths, a colonial bandstand and quiet pavilions fill the gardens between them, for the small moments between the big ones.
