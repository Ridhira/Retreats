04 — Visual principles

How it should feel

Calm, sensory, unhurried. Quiet luxury — restorative, never salesy or flashy.

Editorial & airy
Generous whitespace, hairline borders, full-bleed photography. Let content breathe; near-square corners keep it refined.
Warm imagery
Golden-hour colour-grade, bottom/left scrims for legibility, slow zooms on hover. Water, greenery, stillness.
One accent
Gold alone carries emphasis — rules, dots, hover. Status hues (sage/amber/terracotta) appear only when meaningful.
Signature motifs
The hairline-dash eyebrow, the centred eyebrow with flanking rules, the 48px gold rule, italic-word headlines, numbered cards.

Do

  • Write in sentence case; italic serif for one emphasis word
  • Use cream or charcoal-brown section bands
  • Lift cards with soft shadow + hairline, not heavy borders
  • Keep eyebrows tiny, uppercase, wide-tracked

Don't

  • Use pure black, bright or flashy colours, or emoji
  • Introduce a second accent alongside gold
  • Use large radii or heavy drop-shadows
  • Set body in a competing sans (Inter, Roboto, Arial)
